Method

Walk-forward 4-state Gaussian HMM on (log return, Parkinson range vol, volume-change vol), refit every 21 bars on an expanding window; decisions use only filtered probabilities. Orders decided at the close fill at the next open with spread, square-root impact and commission. Causality is verified in the test suite by corrupting every bar after a cutoff and requiring the equity curve up to the cutoff to be bit-identical; an independent return-space recomputation matches the event-driven ledger to ~1e-15.
